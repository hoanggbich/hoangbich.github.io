02 · Performance

A dip in 2025, then a return to growth

Revenue fell in Q2 and Q3 2025 while the new distribution model was rolled out nationwide, then grew again in Q4. The outcome of the supply chain shows up first in these numbers.

Quarterly net revenue

VND billion · 2024 Q1–Q3 are back-calculated

Insight: Q2 2025 was the low point (−15.1% YoY) during the restructuring; Q4 2025 recovered to +3.7% YoY.

Revenue by category

VND billion · FY2024 vs FY2025

Insight: Seasonings is still the biggest category but shrank 7%. Home & Personal Care (+14%) and Coffee (+3%) grew.

Gross margin by category

% of revenue · higher means more value kept after production costs

Insight: Home & Personal Care added 7.2 points of margin and Coffee added 4.2 points. Convenience Foods lost 2.9 points as input costs rose.

03 · Distribution

Direct Coverage: fewer middlemen, more outlets

In 2024–2025 Masan Consumer moved from a wholesaler-led model to Direct Coverage: its own salesforce now serves retail outlets directly, supported by the Retail Supreme (ASO) ordering app and the Supra logistics platform.

Points of sale by channel

Number of outlets · market size shown in tooltip

Insight: General trade, mostly small family-run shops, still accounts for most outlets, so it decides how well the supply chain works.

Retail Supreme (ASO) members

Thousand retailers ordering digitally

Insight: ASO membership grew 61% in one month (Nov → Dec 2025). The company targets 500K members.

04 · Manufacturing

14 certified factories, North to South

Production is spread across Vietnam's main economic regions, which keeps factories close to raw materials and to consumers. All plants follow FSSC 22000, ISO and HACCP standards.
